Under the hood

What makes it work

🧠
Spaced repetition
SM-2 inspired algorithm schedules every word individually. Words you know come back rarely; words you struggle with come back tomorrow.
🎙️
AI conversation
Chat with Spanish characters tuned to your level. KS gets translations and gentle correction; GCSE gets a Spanish-only examiner running photo cards and role plays.
📝
Sentence builder
Drag-and-tap translation practice with distractors. Four levels at GCSE, scaling from present tense through to subjunctive and Higher-register essay phrases.
📄
Reading comprehension
Levelled passages with vocab glossaries and exam-style questions — multiple choice, true/false, and gap-fill. Foundation and Higher splits at GCSE.
🏫
Live classroom games
Gold Rush and Survival — host a room from your laptop, students join with a code on their phones. Real-time leaderboard. Great as a starter or plenary.
📊
Teacher dashboards
Create classes with join codes. See topic-by-topic progress and time on task per student. No PII collection — usernames only.
